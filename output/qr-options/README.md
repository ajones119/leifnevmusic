# Leif Nevener QR concepts

All three encode the static destination `https://leifnevenermusic.com/`. No tracking service or subscription is involved.

- `01-prairie-print.svg` / `.png`: scissortail on a guitar neck above a large moss QR.
- `02-guitar-label.svg` / `.png`: full acoustic guitar and perched scissortail, with a QR label on the guitar body.
- `03-scissortail-seal.svg` / `.png`: flying scissortail and a prairie guitar framing the code.
- `comparison.png`: all three concepts together, numbered left to right.

SVGs contain vector QR modules and text plus embedded raster illustrations. They are self-contained; the illustrations are not vector outlines. PNGs are rendered from those same compositions.

## Scan verification

The QR matrix is generated by `qrcode` 1.5.4 with error correction H. It retains a four-module quiet zone on each side; artwork does not enter that area. Rounded finder treatment and module corners are stylistic changes to the exact encoded matrix, rather than a generated approximation.

All nine `jsqr` 1.4.0 checks passed: each complete final design at original width, 600px and 400px decoded to the exact intended URL. See `scan-checks.json`. These are digital checks, not physical print/camera validation. Test the selected print at its actual size, material, and lighting before ordering a print run.

Technical reference: [DENSO QR quiet-zone guidance](https://www.qrcode.com/en/howto/code.html).

## Artwork generation

The built-in Image Gen tool produced three independent transparent PNG illustration assets, using `public/media/scissortail-still.png` as the species/style reference. The code and type were composed deterministically afterward; no generated QR pattern is used.

Prompt briefs:

1. Horizontal folk-linocut scissortail perched on an acoustic guitar headstock, neck across the lower illustration, long split tail to the right; moss, amber and cream; transparent; no microphone, lettering or QR.
2. Upright complete front-view acoustic guitar with an uncluttered wooden body, one scissortail perched on its right shoulder and split tail alongside; vintage folk poster illustration; transparent; no lettering or QR.
3. Square organic border with a flying scissortail across the top/right, acoustic guitar and restrained wheat at lower left, central 65% left empty; moss/amber linocut; transparent; no lettering or QR.

The project-local build script is `build.mjs`. QR tooling is isolated in `.scratch/qr-tools/`, not added to the website dependencies. On a fresh checkout, run `npm install --prefix .scratch/qr-tools --no-audit --no-fund` before `node output/qr-options/build.mjs` from the project root to rebuild all exports and repeat digital decoding checks.

These are refinement concepts. No website changes, QR deployment, or git push is included.
